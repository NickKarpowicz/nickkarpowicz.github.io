Ok, here's my policy on CD submissions: they make me feel dirty. Well, most of the time anyway. There have been a few CDs that I've gotten from bands that are really wonderful and I wouldn't have ever heard of them otherwise so it's fun when that happens, but when I get a cd in the mail, I feel obligated to review it, which sucks. I started this site just to talk about the bands that I like and the music that I bought, so I think taking submissions kind of takes away from that. When people who do reviews seek out free cds, it gives the impression that it's more important for them to listen to the music in question than it is for other people, which isn't right. Anyone can write about music, and with the internet, everyone can publish it. I don't think taking the initiative to actually do so entitles anyone to anything.
Anyway, if you're absolutely sure that your cd is really good =), then maybe I'd want to listen to it for free =). But if you're not an indie pop or indie rock band, you won't want to send it to me because I won't like it. I'm pretty broad on my interpretation of indie rock (hardcore japanese to emo to...) but I really despise new school country music. Don't send me new school country music. Please? I mean, one of my rules for my site is that I pretty much only write positive reviews (since it is almost all stuff that I bought, plus negative reviews don't do anyone any good) but when I get a cd in the mail that I'm obligated to review, I get all nervous. Um, that said...

Have fun!
