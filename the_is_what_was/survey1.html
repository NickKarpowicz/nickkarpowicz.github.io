Survey!

Vote to decide who gets the title of Best Freaking Album of All Time!

 Closes on January 23rd!

There are lots of "All Time Top Ten" lists and so on around the world, but I think having ten is a cop out. I mean, to really be honored, you have to be number one anyway, there's no need to mention the others since it just rubs in the fact that they aren't the best and dilutes the victory of the winner. That's why I've started this. This is the end-all to decide which album REALLY is the best. Now, I wouldn't just pick one album and say "this is the best album ever, so ha! My word is law!" No, I've given you, the Big Orange Crayon viewers (both of you), the opportunity to decide. Pick one of these albums, or write in and nominate another, to decide who gets the honor of "Best Freaking Album of All Time". Any mention of "Freebird" will cause me to find you and and kill you.

And try not to vote more than once. That would be silly.
