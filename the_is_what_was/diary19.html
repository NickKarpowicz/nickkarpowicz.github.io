3/23/04
there are fences of wood in Wyoming

So, I was walking down the hall in one of the buildings in school, and I went past some office. I overheard this phrase as I walked by: "plan B is negative Wyoming." I kept walking for a little while, thinking, "oh, plan B is negative Wy... what?" I've been trying to figure out what it could have possibly meant. At first I thought that maybe if something didn't work out, they were going to not go to Wyoming, but when is plan A ever to go to Wyoming? Maybe it's some sort of geography-based algebra and plan A was the cube root of Nebraska.



3/12/04
People won't know what to say

The Smittens show last night was really nice. I bought a CD and now I have all kinds of songs going through my head. Here's a story about a side effect of this:

I was buying groceries today and kind of singing songs to myself as I went along, because why not? Anyway, I was singing the chorus to "Capucine" while digging though the Gardenburger section in Hannaford... and well, the chorus to "Capucine" is "I love my mom so much, I'll give My Little Pony a perfect haircut every time." I wrote diary entries both today and yesterday, which is about 60 times my normal frequency of entries. I thought about the reason for this and that reason is that for a brief little moment, I had something of a life again. Of course, it's back to classes tomorrow, and back to me thinking that nothing I do is interesting enough to bother writing about. That's probably not good. Part of the problem is that there just haven't been many shows going on, and when there were shows going on, I couldn't make it. But really, I resolve to get out of my apartment more. I mean it. No really this time.

I taught myself how to play "Eyelashes" by the Secret Stars today. It's not a hard song, but I'm really bad and playing guitar and singing at the same time. Ok, I'm bad and doing both individually, but when I do them simultaneously, it gets silly. I'm okay at quantum mechanics, though.

12/23/03
Better than nothing

So where have I been the past month?

I had finals through the first half, and then I was finishing up random garbage and then waiting for grades, and all of that is finally over and I'm seeing life as a good thing again. I managed to get straight Bs for the semester. In high school I would have been devastated by something like that, but in grad school, I'm amazed that I don't have to start off recovering from a C. I was really stressed out for a long time because I thought that I was going to get a C or worse in my methods of theoretical physics class, but I think I kicked the final's ass and the professor probably curved the midterm that I bombed. So now I get to do it all again with harder classes and more work! Yay! Seriously, though, for a number of reasons, the past few months have been some of the most awful of my life, and I'm glad that I got through it without becoming jaded or resigned.

Zak is finally back from hitting vegetation with a machete in Hawaii for half a year. It's kind of like things went straight back to normal, except he has a tan and Eric and I are pale New Englanders.

So, uh, Christmas tomorrow, eh? I did all of my shopping (only three presents in total thanks to my sister's divinely inspired secret santa proposal), and I'm going to be seeing the new Lord of the Rings tonight (like last year), so we'll see how that goes. I think I more or less talked my dad into getting me an iPod for my birthday (exactly one month away!), so I'm all excited about materialism for the moment.

Eh, that's all that's coming to mind right now. I'm sure I'm leaving good things out, but you can fill it in as you see fit using the power of your imagination.

11/09/03
Sweet ecclesiastics, won't you preach to me?

Did you ever have a bad week? Sure. I just had me one of them, too. Oh my goodness did I have a bad week. Let me tell you about my bad week, won't you? I'll be succinct.

I got a 39 on my methods of theoretical physics midterm. That's out of a hundred. Yikes. This means that it's mathematically impossible for me to get an A in that class, and very unlikely that I'll get a B (it's worth 25% of the final grade). This sucks, of course. Maybe there will be a curve, maybe there won't be. Along those lines, if you're ever in a graduate physics class and the only book for the course is Jackson's Classical Electrodynamics, buy other books. I'm serious; it's impossible to learn anything from that thing.

My grandfather had a heart attack on Friday. He's ok - he's still in the hospital, but he's doing all right and it was a minor one.

So yeah, not a good week. Lately I've been wondering if I made the right choice being in grad school at all. I mean, yeah I'm completely unemployable as a physics student without it, but I have this unshakable feeling like I don't belong here. Yeah, this mostly came about after I got the midterm back, so maybe if I can recover in that class, I'll fell better, but there's a lot more crap that I have to do before I can just get to doing research, which is the one thing that I'm looking forward to. It's just that if I don't jump through the hoops well enough, I won't get there, and who knows what I'd do then. Besides be poor.

Ahh, but it's all right, though. Things usually work out, I just have to figure out what to do and go from there.

Did you see the moon last night? It was red.

Just to see what the freezer can show me

Well, this was an interesting week.

It certainly started off awesomely. I went to see Ted Leo and the Malarkies at the Flywheel and when I was there I met up with Jodi, who I hadn't seen in some ridiculous amount of time. This all made me get home past my bedtime and sleepy in class the next day, but whatever.

Things kind of went downhill from there.

On Tuesday night, I was up in the grad student offices grading assignments, when I saw the largest cockroach I have ever seen crawl by my desk. I was kind of intrigued/repulsed, so I kind of wheeled my chair over to watch where it was going. Then I felt a tug on my ear and realized that I was wearing headphones and they were connected to my iBook, which was then connecting its face with the concrete floor. The only thing that was broken was the hard drive, at least. I finally got the replacement drive that I bought today, and holy shit is it hard to get around inside of an iBook. I got it in, and everything seems fine now, but oh my goodness.

I've been kind of buried by coursework this week, too. I was on campus for fourteen hours on Wednesday just trying to get my quantum mechanics homework done. But now I know what's up, so I've got plans, so it'll be easier.
