Dan and Liz from Ida
You Are My Flower
Benefit for Mothers and Others

So, do we need a CD of the two original members of Ida playing 12 childrens' songs? Yes. Yes we do. Sure, nobody's going to appreciate it in the same way as a proper Ida album, but the whole idea has this incredible sweetness to it that even though you've heard most of the songs before, they're incredibly simple, and your kindergarten teacher probably did the same thing three times in the course of a week, it still feels worth every penny (that goes to a good cause anyway).

For me, it all peaks with the so-precious-that-you-find-yourself-looping-it-and-giggling "Freight Train" ("Goin' to Brooklyn goin' so fast!"). I was just beside myself the first time I heard it. I guess I'm just sappy. Really, the album has a goofy weekend feel to it, which makes sense since it was all recorded on one Saturday.

Ok, so who would really buy this? Ida obsessives, of course. Or maybe you want to give your little cousin some indie-cred or feel like the music in your life has just gone downhill since you learned "Jingle Bells" or something. In any case, I think I can safely say that every single one of us that crazy enough to actually buy this CD will love it.

Total score: 618 out of 620
