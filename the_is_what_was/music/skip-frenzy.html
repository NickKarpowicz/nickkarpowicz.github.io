Big Orange Crayon

musical things

Skip Frenzy
El Proto In Soho
Round Records (self produced)

This disc has three very important things: it has good songs, it is short, and it is cheap. If you go for the tape instead of the CD it's even free! So you don't have anything to lose, really. But get the CD. Its only five dollars, so be nice.

This is their first recording, and it's really promising. Although it only has three songs, they show a pretty good range, with the first song being pretty soft comparatively and the last being more rocky. Sonically, I place them somewhere around the Stone Roses, with a bit of an emo influence. Which means its immediately enjoyable and complex. Which is good.

So, go to their site, download the whole thing in MP3s, or buy the CD, or get the free tape and listen. You should like it. If you don't, all you lost is time, and if you do, well, then you know about another band that you like, don't you? Yes, you do.
