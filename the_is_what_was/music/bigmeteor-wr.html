Big Meteor
Wild River

David Wimble must be from the part of Canada that's right in between Tennessee and Alabama. His band, Big Meteor, which constists of him, has a pretty eclectic sound that it somewhere around Roy Orbison and, um, other stuff. It is what is referred to as "Rock and Roll". Remember that? I don't mean that silly pop rock and roll that became big in the 70's with Foghat and shit, I mean the kind of rock and roll that the aforementioned Roy Orbison played, as well as those older groups that always get listed as influences upon more fameous bands. The kind of rock and roll that isn't afraid to admit it's country influences and holds the blues in the highest regard. Which I guess is kinda weird since he's a Canadian and the majority of music coming from the Southern States that gets played on the radio nowadays is either generic alternarock or wife-beating country music.

But even going beyond geography, this is a cool album. It is, as I said in quite a long-winded manner in the preceding paragraph, rock and roll, but is not confined to the Grand Funk Railroad limitations that I normally associate with stuff that is categorized as rock and roll these days (not punk, not pop rock, not alternarock, just rock and roll). The songs don't sound like you've heard them on classic rock stations before, new ground gets broken here, or at least retread in a way which hasn't been done well for a long time. And the lyrics are intelligent and poetic, in a rustic, universally human kind of way (" To a prisoner the stars seem humble in the silence / children walking along the light / like waters rising above an island / my eternal soul will guide me tonight" - from "Honest Man").

Although this is not the type of music I generally listen to, this is an excellent album and deserves a listen or fifty. If you want to check it out, there are lots of MP3 and RealAudio music samples on the Big Meteor web page. You won't be disappointed. I'm not. And also, this CD was the first CD that I ever got sent for free for the purposes of reviewing on this page, which is a really super thing which I encourage every band in the world to do.

Total score: 412 out of 412
