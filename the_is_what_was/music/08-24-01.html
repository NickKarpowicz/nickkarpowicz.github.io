Ladyfest (many bands, including K. and the Naysayer)
Easthampton Town Hall (by way of The Flywheel)
August 24th, 2001

This was an awesome show, but to do a review would take a lot more time than I have right now since it's the first day of classes and there were seven bands. I had a good time, though. I ended up having to go it alone, but still. The show got out at a little after one in the morning and it's a two hour drive home, but it was still worth it.

Here are the pictures, anyway. I'm not quite sure of the names of some of the bands, so my apologies if I get them wrong.
