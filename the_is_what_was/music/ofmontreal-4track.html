musical things

Of Montreal
The Early Four Track Recordings
Kindercore Records

It's impossible not to love an album where the song titles form a story about Dustin Hoffman. Yeah, the album kicks off with "Dirty Dustin Hoffman needs a bath" which is followed by "Dustin Hoffman gets a bath", "Dustin Hoffman thinkss about eating the soap" and so on. For some reason, things like this don't really suprise me coming from Of Montreal. If, say, Fugazi suddenly started naming all their songs after Erik Estrada, then maybe it would get a jolt out of me, but for a band like Of Montreal that has more songs with insects as main characters than any other I can think of, it makes sense. I don't know what to make of the fact that they've streched the definition of "making sense" so far, though.

Anyway, the things with the names are also songs, and that aspect is probably more worthy of attention. Oddly enough, this is the most "normal" Of Montreal album I've heard so far. Sure, it's off-beat and all that, but it's not quite as insane as their newer stuff. Since it is a collection of early four track recordings, there isn't the bizarre orchestration of The Gay Parade and so on, it's mostly just Kevin Barnes and some guitar and drum parts mixed in with just a few strange gloopy noises. Really, the whole thing is pleasant, although rough-sounding and lo-fi, 60's-style pop songs.

And, um, I really like "Dustin Hoffman does not resist the temptation to eat the bathtub". I think it's very pretty. As strange as that sounds...
