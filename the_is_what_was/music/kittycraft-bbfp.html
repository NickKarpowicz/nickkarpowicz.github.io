Kitty Craft
Beats and Breaks from the Flower Patch
Kindercore Records

Who would have thought that indie pop and hip-hop would go so well together? (Besides rhyming)

They really do! I mean it! I have proof! Buy this!

It has a cute little cat eyeing a cute little bee on the cover. Its on Kindercore records. The band is named Kitty Craft. Yeah, this is indie pop.

But there are scratchy samples and hip hop beats and stuff like that, too. Indiehop? Hip-pop? Whatever. I like it. Its cool and happy at the same time. Neat!

What else to say? Um... nice girl vocals sung over the whole thing (no rappers or guys grunting and going "ungh!" over and over again in the background. Bonus!). Uh... goes down smooth and fresh? Sure.
