Three Point Turn and Jupiter Sunrise
Valentine's downstars, Albany, NY
May 12th, 2002

A weird thing happened at this show: Ben's stepmom (Ben from Jupiter Sunrise) came up to me and asked, "do you have a sister named Jen?" And I don't have a sister named Jen, but after some more questions, I figured out that it was my friend Jen that she was talking about. And she had never seen Jen and I together... I guess we have similar hair...
