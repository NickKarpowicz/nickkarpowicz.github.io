Rival of the Cold
Brentwood Estates

Nick [to himself]: don't call it emo don't call it emo don't call it emo...

Ok, yeah, it's tempting to call this emo, if only for the fact that the most immediate things that it reminds me of are Hum and Jets to Brazil, but that's not what it's all about. It's indie-pop, but not so pure and simple. It definitely has the rockier feel of the afforementioned bands plus it has a pleasantly airy, poppy feel. The feeling of depth is there, but it's not chokingly dense or lacking of hooks and such. As far as the singing goes, the guy makes me think of Blake Schwarzenbach, but he doesn't do that grating creaky screech thing, so it's all good.
