Eric Metronome
The Two-Eared Man
BBPTC

Eric Rottmayer is one of the most brilliant songwriters alive today. Yeah, that's a bold statement that I can't really back up, but it's how I feel. The terrifying thing is that every successive release is better than the last. This one, for example, takes the catchy, introspective, and beautiful songwriting of the Lime Green cassette and the She EP, adds better recording techniques and forms some stunning pop songs. I guess to get a feel for what it sounds like, you could picture the Secret Stars but a bit poppier, or maybe Kincaid or Masters of the Hemisphere or one of those Kindercore bands, but warmer and more personal.

I think "Waiting for Fall" is one of the most beautiful songs I'm going to hear in a long time. I actaully had a tear in my eye the first time I heard it. I know I'm not the most hardcore person on the planet, but damn!

"Blur the Thoughts" "a.m. in the A.M.", "Suicides Go To Hell", "Nobody Knows What Love Is (Love Always Ends" and pretty much the rest of the album is equally wonderful. I had the whole thing memorized within a week of owning the CD. Things like that don't happen all that often.
