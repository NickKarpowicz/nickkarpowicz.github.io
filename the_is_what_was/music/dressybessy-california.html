Dressy Bessy
The California EP
Kindercore Records

It's been a long time since I've written a music review and even longer since I've written a straight out "yay indie pop!" one. So...

Yay Dressy Bessy!

Sometimes it's easy to get swept up in the overseriousness of your Kid As and your The Moon and Antarticas and forget that the coolest thing that music can do is cheer you up and make you want to jump around a bit. Sure, that other stuff is fine for those days when you want to talk someone into thinking that you're all smart and serious, but I'm neither so I can put on some Dressy Bessy and sing along in a terrible falsetto and cheer up. The mix of fuzzy/jangly guitars and Tammy Ealom's airy, cheerful voice makes it impossible to frown.

I've been waiting for a new Dressy Bessy album for a long time, and while this is an EP, is does the job for now. The title track is criminally catchy, just like you would expect from them, and the rest of the album goes on in that vain, climaxing, for me, with "Super * Everything" which is another silly fun shout along. Things get a bit strange at the end with "In the Morning" but it doesn't ever stop being fun.

Total score: 743 out of 743
