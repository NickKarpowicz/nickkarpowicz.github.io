The Pony in the Pancake, The Frank Boscoes, and Missy Bly
Valentine's, Albany, NY
March 5th, 2005

This show was put together by Laura, who does the radio show Hello Pretty City. It was really nice! Yay for pop bands coming to Albany!

The Pony in the Pancake played about ten million fun little songs that lasted for upwards of ten seconds each.

It's kinda neat: I met Frank Boscoe before at a Cynthia Nelson show at the Flywheel a while back, and I thought, "hey nice, a person from Albany who likes some of the same music that I do," not knowing that I owned one of his CDs (Territory Songs by the Gazetteers, which I loved and used to play a lot on my radio show).

Missy Bly is a rock band (person?) for Burlington, VT. I might even go so far as to call her music Cuddlecore because it reminds me of those bands!
