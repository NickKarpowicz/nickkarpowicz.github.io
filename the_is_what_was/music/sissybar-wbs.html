Sissybar
"Smiley (We Become)" 7"
Paris Caramel

Stop reading this. Go buy this 7". You can get it at The Twee Kitten. I'm serious. Your life is hollow and empty otherwise.

I think I just spent about an hour listening to this 7" over and over again and I'm still doing it.

I like it a whole bunch, you see.

The a-side, "Smiley (We Become)" is a totally fun, really fast almost punk-ish pop song and it's so so so great. It kinda reminds me of that REM song, but with mainly girl vocals and more going on and its faster and cooler. The flip is "Student Body Treasurer" and its cool, too. Its slower and kinda softer, and it's really neat and has all sorts of weird nintendo noises going on in the background, behind the guy/girl vocals and undistorted guitars and keyboards and stuff.

Total score: 792 out of 792
