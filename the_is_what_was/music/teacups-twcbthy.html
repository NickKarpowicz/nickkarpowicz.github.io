The Teacups
This Will Come Back to Haunt You
Kindercore Records

More from my indie pop kick. These guys are very much in line with the rest of the genre, but with a bit of a twist. The guy's vocals (yep, a guy and a girl sing) are less treble and a whole lot more bass than the rest. Which is a bit unnerving at first, actually. But it grows on you.

It's fun. It's light. "red light, green light" is really silly. They toured with Wolfie (why not near me?! :-( Oh well.) It has all the simple, pure guitar chords you want, with some keyboard melodies and everything that makes this kind of stuff fun.

Total score: 324 out of 335
