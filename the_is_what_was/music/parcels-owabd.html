The Parcels
"Oh, What a Busy Day" 7"
Brentwood Estates

I'm running out of way to say "I like this because it's sweet, simple, fun indie pop." Well, it applies for this 7" as much as it does for anything else, so I might as well say it. There are three nice, fast, happy pop songs on this record and they're all enjoyable to listen to. If you like Wolfie and/or The I Live the Life of A Movie Star Secret Hideout, you will probably dig this too. It has sweet girl vocals and undistorted guitars and nice, simple drum beats and bass lines. The lyrics mainly involve dreaming and having fun, which fits in just fine with the music. As I've said about a billion other things, if you like happy music, you will like this. If not, here's another thing in life that you don't like.
